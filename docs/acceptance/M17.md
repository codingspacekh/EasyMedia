## Step 2: performance with an animation open

`tests/e2e/animation-performance.spec.ts` checks the budget in the real app with
`tests/helpers/heavy-animation.ts`: a 1024 × 768 stage, 10 s at 30 fps, 100 objects of every drawn
kind (rectangles, ellipses, stars, polygons, text, pen shapes; a third with gradients) on five
layers, each moving, turning and fading all the time, so every frame redraws all of them.

| Check | Budget | MacBook (Apple Silicon), before → after |
|---|---|---|
| N1 the stage playing, frames really painted (the playhead read at each screen refresh) | ≥ 30 fps | 28.3–30 fps with 70–190 ms stalls → 30 fps, longest gap 18 ms in 9 of 10 runs |
| N2 a 10 s MP4 of it, Export to "Saved" | ≤ 30 s | 15.6 s (GPU canvas) → 13–14 s: 11 s drawing the 300 frames, 2 s FFmpeg |
| N4 ten 1080p clips on the video timeline, played, then the animation played through | ≤ 600 MB, all processes | 648 MB → 501 MB (video alone 412 MB) |
| N5 start-up, from the performance report | ≤ 5 s | 0.9 s |

Found and fixed:

- **The stage made its canvas again on every frame**: its size was set at each redraw, which throws
  the pixels away and allocates a new canvas (2048 × 1536 on a Retina screen). Now it is set only
  when the stage, the zoom or the screen changes (`sizeCanvas` in `stage-geometry.ts`), and
  otherwise the canvas is `reset()`. The stalls went.
- **The graphics card's memory filled up while playing or exporting**: Chromium keeps the shapes of
  every frame drawn on a GPU canvas in its caches: the GPU process grew from 75 MB to 394 MB over
  10 s of playback, and to 342 MB over an export. The stage and the export now draw in ordinary
  memory (`willReadFrequently`): the GPU process stays at about 75 MB; the page grows 33 MB more
  than before. The page's own work per frame did not change (about 7.5 ms on the MacBook).
- **Exporting draws four frames at once** (`drawFrames`, `FRAMES_AT_ONCE` in
  `app/animation/exporting.ts`): each frame's PNG is made on another thread while the next is
  drawn: 12–17 s → 11 s of drawing. After Cancel or an error no frame starts, and the frames
  already started are waited for before the frames are removed. Frames drawn at once load the
  pictures once (`stage-renderer.ts`).

Still to watch (the teacher's laptop decides):

- **An export's peak memory**: the main process grows by about 90 MB and the page by about 80 MB
  while the frames pass through (it does not grow again on a second or third export: the memory is
  kept for reuse, not leaked). With the video project open as well, the peak during an animation
  export would be over 600 MB on the MacBook's count. If the teacher's report shows that, the next
  step is to send the frames to FFmpeg as raw pictures instead of PNG files.
- **The export time on a slow laptop**: 13–14 s here, most of it making the PNGs; a laptop three
  times slower would be over 30 s. The same change would remove that work.
- macOS counts memory differently from Windows (shared pages included), so the teacher's Windows
  report is the figure that counts for N4.

## The 9626/04 exam papers in the app (2026-11-03)

Three real Task 3s (`documents/exam_papers`, not in git: M/J 2025 Race, F/M 2026 AND gate, M/J
2026 Gas and Oxidation) were done in the app through its UI, as a student would, and exported
(`*_ZZ999_9999`). All three came out as the papers ask, except as noted. Found:

- **Fixed:** pasting into another animation left its pictures behind (the atoms vanished from the
  gas cloud): the clipboard now carries the pictures the objects show, and a paste reuses one the
  animation already has, by its id or its file, and adds the others, stop motion's too
  (`pasteObjects` in `core/animation/layers.ts`).
- **Fixed:** a pasted copy was named twice ("Box 2 2"); it is now named once against the animation
  it goes into ("Box 2", "Box 3"; in another animation it keeps its name).
- **Open (a feature):** the stage has no transparent background, so no PNG with transparency:
  the Gas task's PNG has a white background.
- **Open:** a 24 fps animated GIF plays 4 % fast (a GIF's frame times are whole hundredths of a
  second: 40 ms); 25 fps is exact. The tasks were done at 25 fps.
- **Open:** a group's opacity is applied to each of its parts, so overlapping parts look darker;
  the cloud was drawn as one Pen shape instead.
- The papers' `Stream1.png`, `Stream2.png` and `ANDGateAnimation.mp4` were not supplied: the
  streams were typed as text (Courier Prime) moving under a mask layer.

## Twelve more 9626/04 papers in the app (2026-11-03)

`documents/exam_papers2` (not in git): 6 animation, 2 audio, 4 video tasks from 2023–26, done
through the UI (`*_ZZ999_9999`). Done: Orbit (motion path, the light side turned to the Sun), Tier
logo (9 SVG pieces, two turned), Cake message (mask wipes), Conveyor belt (BasicWheel drawn here,
white background), all the audio steps, and the four video storyboards. Skipped: the Apple (all
.svg). Found (the three bugs fixed 2026-11-04):

- **Fixed:** typing a text's Size at a later frame made its box twice scaled (W 1264 instead of
  452): Properties keys the measured W and H with the size, and the size keyframe scaled them
  again. A text's size track is now applied first (`objectAt` in `core/animation/tween.ts`), so
  keyed W and H give the box exactly.
- **Fixed:** Export audio picked Mono when every track was mono, even with two tracks panned hard
  left and right: it now starts as Stereo when a track is stereo or panned
  (`AudioExportDialog.svelte`).
- **Fixed:** Snapshot with the playhead inside the video's last frame (e.g. 9.99 s of 10 s at
  30 fps; where the clip starts does not matter): "Something went wrong". The snapshot seeks in
  the finished video, and FFmpeg keeps the first frame at or after the time, so there was none.
  Inside the last frame it now seeks half a frame before that frame starts; any other time is
  kept (`seekTo` in `core/render/compile.ts`; checked with FFmpeg in `render-look.test.ts`).
- **Missing:** SVG export (Apple task); OGG export (May 24 audio); a transparent stage (Conveyor);
  a custom speed such as 1.25× (May 26 video); resizing a picture without cropping; a picture over
  a picture in Video mode; exporting at a source's own size when it is not 16:9 or 4:3 (852 × 480);
  typing a clip's start time in Audio mode.
- Not supplied by the papers: BasicWheel, MyVideo_ (stand-ins made), Stream1/2 (first set).
