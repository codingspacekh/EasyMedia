1. **Learn menu**: the mode switch shows Video, Audio, Animation; **Learn ▸ Inspector & Sampling / Formats & Compression / Animation Theory / Task Checker** each fill the window; **Back to …** returns to the editor you were in; **Learn ▸ Glossary** opens over an editor.
2. **Panels**: in each editor drag a panel's edge wider and narrower (and the timeline taller); double-click the edge; hide a panel with its small arrow and show it again from the strip; **View ▸ Show / Hide Left Panel / Right Panel / Timeline** (Ctrl+Alt+1/2/3); quit and reopen — the layout is the same; **View ▸ Reset Layout**. Try it on the smallest laptop screen.
3. **"Why use this?"**: open a few boxes (Inspector, Audio effects, Animation panels) and hover toolbar buttons: a bold short answer, then the reasons as points, then the syllabus point. Is it easier to read?
4. **Library** (tool strip or K): place a cartoon, an icon, a flowchart shape and a background; search "weather"; Ungroup the car and turn a wheel on its own.
5. **Effects**: select an object, move the playhead, Properties ▸ Effects ▸ Fly in from the left, 1 s ▸ Add effect; try Typewriter on text, Pulse, Spin; see the keyframes on the timeline; Undo; export an MP4.

## Decisions and notes

- **Learn fills the window** (the teacher's choice) rather than a window or panel of its own: simplest on small laptop screens. The editors keep their state while Learn is shown.
- **Panel sizes include their 4 px edge**; a panel can shrink below its saved size down to its smallest when the window is narrow, so the work area is never squeezed out.
- **"Why use this?" keeps the registry's words** (the exam's wording); only the arrangement changes. One answer that ran over the length limit (Snapshot) was split into two sentences.
- **The library is drawn for EasyMedia** from the app's own shapes, so it carries no third-party licence and every part stays editable; backgrounds stretch to the stage's shape.
- **Effects only add keyframes**, so students see the tween on the timeline and can change it — the effect is a quick start, not a black box. Fades are even; fly-ins slow as they land.
- **Fixed (N10):** with the clearer, taller "Why use this?" boxes, five older dialogs (Snapshot, Audio effects, Licences, Stage settings, Animation export) could run below a small screen with no way to reach their buttons (CI on Windows and Apple Silicon). Every modal dialog now stays inside the window and scrolls (`ui/theme.css`); the whole end-to-end suite passes in a 1100 × 700 window (`E2E_SIZE=1100x700`).
- **The teacher's layout review (2026-11-03)**, with a screenshot: the stage now stays in the middle of its area whatever the panels' sizes and the zoom; every form control has one look from `ui/forms.css` (text, number and search boxes, menus with the app's arrow, text areas, sliders — vertical ones too —, colour pickers, checkboxes and radio buttons), with focus shown inside the border so it never covers a unit; the per-component copies were removed; "Why use this?" is a link whose explanation is a tooltip on the page body above everything (dialogs included), shown on hover or focus, pinned by a click, closed by Escape or a click elsewhere, so it never moves the layout; the last three inline why boxes and six dialogs without Escape were brought in line.
- **Not done in this refactor** (left for M17, as they work and are well tested): splitting `app/audio.ts` (1 196 lines) and the large Stage and Timeline components in the same way, and moving the older dialogs onto `Dialog`.
