## Decisions and notes

- **Stop motion is one picture object with held `picture` keyframes**, not a frame-by-frame layer of pictures: the whole clip moves and resizes as one, as photographs from one camera should. Frame-by-frame layers are for drawn work (flip book, cel), with onion skin.
- **Frame-by-frame keeps the objects flat on the layer**, each marked with its drawing's keyframe (`drawing`), and the layer lists its keyframes (`drawings`). Every existing command (move, group, delete, paste, Properties) works unchanged; only what is shown at a frame is filtered.
- **An edit on a still drawing changes the drawing**, not a tween keyframe (as Animate: a drawing is held until the next keyframe). A drawing's object that has its own keyframes still tweens.
- **The schema stays 4**: buttons came after v1.3.0, so no released app has read version 4; the new fields are optional.
- **F6 / F7** follow Animate (Insert Keyframe / Insert Blank Keyframe).
- **Learn ▸ Animation is written in our own words** (the coursebook is copyright), covering every point its questions ask; the acceptance test checks the mark-scheme points, not the wording.
- **A GIF cannot store 24 fps exactly** (its frame times are hundredths of a second: 24 fps is kept as 25); the task checker accepts the nearest a GIF can store.
- **A PNG sequence has no frame rate of its own**: the checker counts its pictures against the task's length × frame rate.
- Fixed in a test, not the app: reading a pixel of a yuv420p video needs RGB before the 1 × 1 crop.
- **Fixed (N10):** the timeline was a fixed 190 px, so when its bar wrapped onto two lines on a small window (1100 × 700, or CI's screens once the Frame-by-frame button was added) only about four rows showed and a row scrolled into view sat under the sticky ruler. The rows area now keeps its height and the timeline grows with its bar (the stage fits what is left); an end-to-end test at 1100 × 700 keeps it so.
