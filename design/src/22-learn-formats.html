Files vs containers. MP4, MOV, AVI and WAV are containers: they hold compressed streams plus information about them. The codec inside decides whether the data is lossy or lossless.

More pixels per frame, more frames per second (30 fps needs twice the data of 15 fps) and a longer duration all increase the size. Lossy compression shrinks it, but too much causes blocky, blurred pictures.
